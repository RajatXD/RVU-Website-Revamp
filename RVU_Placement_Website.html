Placement Process

The 10-step recruitment process is retained from the RVU placement information rather than replacing it with a generic six-step journey.

01

Registration

Company registration with the placement function.

02

Requirement Discussion

Discuss hiring requirements and role details.

03

JD Approval

Job description is reviewed and approved.

04

Student Registration

Eligible students register for the opportunity.

05

Shortlisting

Applications are screened and candidates shortlisted.

06

Online Test

Assessment conducted where applicable.

07

Technical Interview

Role-specific technical assessment.

08

HR Interview

HR discussion and final evaluation.

09

Offer

Offer is released to selected candidates.

10

Joining

Joining is coordinated with the selected candidate.

Placement Governance

Rules & Regulations

Key placement governance information is retained as part of the recruiter and student process.

Placement Governance

  • Corporate & Alumni Relations (CAR) coordinates placement and internship activities across Schools.
  • CAR facilitates processes but does not adjudicate disciplinary matters.
  • Placement-related violations are formally referred to the Student Disciplinary Committee (STDC).

Student Eligibility

  • No academic backlogs at the time of registering for placement drives.
  • Minimum 80% attendance in mandatory pre-placement training.
  • Submission of the Placement Registration Form.

Pre-Placement Training

  • Domain / Technical Training (School-specific)
  • Soft Skills Training
  • Emotional, Behaviour & Networking Intelligence
  • Minimum 80% attendance across all components is compulsory.

Student Responsibilities

  • Apply only to roles the student is genuinely willing to join.
  • Maintain professional conduct at all stages.
  • Honour offers once selected, in line with institutional ethics.
