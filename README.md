 
RV UNIVERSITY 
Placement Website Redesign 
Complete Change & Improvement Document 
 
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY 
What this redesign changes 
The redesign turns the placement experience into a clearer, placement first portal. The website now 
brings decision critical information closer to the surface, connects the recruiter journey from talent 
discovery to CAR contact, and uses interactive components to reduce information overload. 
• Clear placement first hierarchy and navigation. 
• Prominent placement outcomes and key metrics. 
• Multidisciplinary eligible student pool presented by School and programme. 
• Actual 10 step recruitment process retained and made scannable. 
• Internships, live projects, mentoring and industry engagement grouped into an interactive experience. 
• Placement report archive created for previous academic years. 
• Recruiter categories and selected recruiter names made visible. 
• Placement governance, eligibility, training and student responsibilities retained. 
• Direct Corporate & Alumni Relations contact path and recruiter enquiry modal. 
• Responsive mobile navigation, layouts and tables. 
• Smooth anchor navigation plus active section scroll tracking. 
• Accessibility minded interaction details such as labels, roles, selected states and keyboard Escape 
handling. 
• Data integrity guardrails for unverified salary distribution visuals and unavailable historical PDFs.

DETAILED IMPROVEMENTS 
1. Information architecture & navigation 
Placement first navigation 
What was improved: A dedicated jump navigation exposes Overview, Why RVU, Talent Pool, Process, 
Internships, Placements, Reports, Recruiters and Contact. 
Why it matters: Users can reach high intent placement information without scrolling through unrelated 
university content. 
Quick link strip 
What was improved: An Explore Placements row provides direct links to Placement Process, Eligible 
Student Pool, Internships & Industry Exposure, Placement Outcomes, Placement Reports and Recruiter 
Network. 
Why it matters: It creates a fast path for recruiters, students and parents who already know what they are 
looking for. 
 
Active section feedback 
What was improved: IntersectionObserver logic updates the active jump navigation item as the user 
scrolls. 
Why it matters: The navigation behaves more like a real portal and gives users orientation within a long 
page. 
Smooth scrolling with header offset 
What was improved: Anchor links calculate an offset before scrolling and update the URL hash. 
Why it matters: Sticky headers no longer obscure the section heading after a jump. 
2. Homepage / hero 
Sharper placement proposition 
What was improved: The hero leads with “Empowering Industry Innovators with Top Tier Talent,” 
identifies Corporate & Alumni Relations and highlights a 1,600+ multidisciplinary talent pool. 
Why it matters: A recruiter can understand the purpose of the page and RVU's core talent proposition 
immediately. 
Action oriented hero CTAs 
What was improved: The hero offers “Recruit from RVU” and “View Placement Outcomes.” 
Why it matters: The first screen gives users both an action path and an evidence path. 
  
 
Prominent placement metrics 
What was improved: 425+ placement offers, 250+ recruiters, ₹43.5 LPA highest package and ₹8.0 LPA 
average package are placed directly below the hero. 
Why it matters: High value evidence is visible early instead of being buried in the page.

3. Why Recruit at RV University 
Six recruiter facing value propositions 
What was improved: Industry Ready Talent, Diverse Talent Pool, Industry Integrated Learning, Future 
Focused Curriculum, Seamless Recruitment Support and Strong Academic Excellence are presented as 
scannable cards. 
Why it matters: The section translates broad university strengths into concise reasons a recruiter may 
care about. 
CAR positioning 
What was improved: Corporate & Alumni Relations is explicitly positioned as the function coordinating

Why it matters: This gives recruiters a clear institutional owner for the recruitment journey. 
 
4. Talent Pool 
School level talent discovery 
What was improved: The eligible pool is presented as a 1,608 student total with School level counts 
across SoCSE, SoEB, SDI, SoLAS, SoFMCA and SoL. 
Why it matters: Recruiters can quickly see where relevant talent sits before opening programme level 
detail. 
Programme level detail 
What was improved: Programme figures supplied for the redesign are shown in a structured table. 
Why it matters: The site supports both quick scanning and deeper talent discovery. 
Multidisciplinary positioning 
What was improved: Engineering, economics and business, design, liberal arts and sciences, film/media 
and law are presented together. 
Why it matters: This is a distinctive RVU proposition and becomes a central recruiter benefit rather than 
background copy. 
  
 
5. Placement Process 
Actual 10 step workflow retained 
What was improved: Registration → Requirement Discussion → JD Approval → Student Registration 
→ Shortlisting → Online Test → Technical Interview → HR Interview → Offer → Joining. 
Why it matters: The redesign preserves the supplied RVU workflow rather than replacing it with a 
generic recruitment diagram. 
Visual process timeline 
What was improved: The ten steps are arranged as two five step rows with numbered nodes and concise 
descriptions. 
Why it matters: The full process is understandable at a glance without losing operational detail. 
Recruitment CTA 
What was improved: A “Begin Recruitment with CAR” action sits directly after the process. 
Why it matters: The user can move from understanding the workflow to initiating a conversation.

9. Recruiter Network 
Recruiter categories 
What was improved: MNCs, GCCs, Tech, Consulting, Financial and Startups are displayed as a

Why it matters: The user gets a quick picture of the types of organizations represented. 
Selected recruiter names

10. Rules, governance & student responsibilities 
Governance made discoverable 
What was improved: CAR coordination, disciplinary boundaries, STDC referral, eligibility, pre 
placement training and student responsibilities are retained. 
Why it matters: Operational information is available to students and recruiters instead of being omitted

Eligibility clarity 
What was improved: No academic backlogs, minimum 80% attendance in mandatory pre placement 
training and placement registration are stated. 
Why it matters: Students can understand important participation requirements before engaging with 
drives. 
  
 
11. Recruiter enquiry & CAR contact 
Direct CAR contact 
What was improved: The contact section provides placements@rvu.edu.in and the CAR office address. 
Why it matters: Recruiters have a clear institutional contact point. 
Recruitment enquiry modal 
What was improved: The Job Announcement Form captures organisation name, HR email, 
school/cohort, role and recruitment requirement. 
Why it matters: The prototype demonstrates what a future recruiter intake flow could look like. 
Prototype honesty 
What was improved: The form explicitly states that it does not transmit data and confirms local capture 
only. 
Why it matters: The interaction demonstrates the UX without pretending that a backend integration 
exists. 
12. Responsive / mobile improvements 
Mobile navigation 
What was improved: A mobile menu button opens a collapsible navigation and closes after a link is 
selected. 
Why it matters: The full placement navigation remains usable on smaller screens. 
Responsive layouts 
What was improved: Multi column grids collapse to one or two columns depending on viewport width; 
the process becomes a vertical sequence; tables become horizontally scrollable. 
Why it matters: The same information architecture survives on mobile rather than being squeezed into 
desktop layouts. 
Mobile typography and spacing 
What was improved: Hero typography, statistics, logo dimensions, cards and section spacing are 
reduced at mobile breakpoints. 
Why it matters: The page remains readable and balanced on narrow screens. 
 
13. Visual design system 
Consistent RVU inspired visual language 
What was improved: Gold, dark slate, white and soft neutral surfaces create a consistent palette; 
Playfair Display is used for major headings and Montserrat for interface text. 
Why it matters: The site feels like a coherent institutional placement experience rather than a collection 
of unrelated components. 
  
Card hierarchy 
What was improved: Rounded cards, restrained borders and subtle shadows separate content groups 
without excessive decoration. 
Why it matters: The design remains premium while preserving readability. 
Consistent CTA language 
What was improved: Primary actions use a small set of visual button styles. 
Why it matters: Users can distinguish actions from informational content quickly. 
14. Technical / interaction improvements 
• Responsive viewport configuration and mobile breakpoints. 
• Smooth anchor navigation with header offset. 
• IntersectionObserver based active navigation state. 
• Mobile navigation state with aria expanded. 
• Modal open/close behavior, backdrop close and Escape to close. 
• Interactive internship and industry ecosystem detail modals. 
• Tab state management using active classes and aria selected. 
• Form validation through required fields and email input type. 
• Prototype only submission feedback instead of fake server transmission. 
• Inactive historical report handling with explicit user feedback. 
• Confirm the eligible student total and programme level counts are internally reconciled before 
publication. 
• Self contained HTML/CSS/JS prototype suitable for competition demonstration. 
DATA INTEGRITY 
What should be verified before production 
The redesign intentionally distinguishes between design presentation and verified institutional data. 
Before public deployment, RVU/CAR should confirm the figures and replace prototype placeholders 
with official documents or live integrations. 
• Confirm all current placement metrics and package figures with the official CAR source. 
• Verify the salary distribution visual's denominator and meaning before publishing percentage labels. 
• Replace the inactive 2024–25 and 2023–24 report buttons with official PDFs when supplied. 
 
• Replace prototype recruiter enquiry handling with the approved official form/backend if the site goes 
live. 
• Confirm contact details and any university wide footer links before production launch. 
COMPETITION POSITIONING 
Why these changes make the redesign stronger 
• It is not only a visual refresh; it changes how placement information is discovered. 
• It gives recruiters a clear path from talent pool → process → engagement → contact. 
• It gives students a fast path to process, opportunities, resources and outcomes. 
• It gives parents clearer visibility into outcomes and the broader ecosystem. 
• It preserves the actual 10 step process and supplied institutional terminology. 
• It treats transparency as a UX feature by creating a report archive and avoiding fabricated evidence. 
• It demonstrates interaction, responsiveness and information architecture rather than relying only on 
static screens.
